## How to choose a contractor in Los Angeles

1. **Confirm an active CSLB license first.** California requires a licensed contractor for any job over $1,000 (raised from $500 in January 2025 under AB 2622). Look it up on the [CSLB site](https://www.cslb.ca.gov), do not take their word for it.
2. **Match the license class to the job.** A kitchen, bath, or addition touches two or more trades, so you want a Class B general building contractor, not a single specialty license.
3. **Get the permit in the plan.** Anything with plumbing, gas, electrical, or a layout change needs an [LADBS](https://www.ladbs.org) permit and inspection. A contractor who skips permits is the one to walk away from.
4. **Weigh budget against resale.** A minor kitchen remodel returns most of its cost at resale. Ask for an itemized bid, not a single number.
5. **Ask about the exact project.** How many kitchens or ADUs like yours have they finished in the last two years, and can you talk to those homeowners.
